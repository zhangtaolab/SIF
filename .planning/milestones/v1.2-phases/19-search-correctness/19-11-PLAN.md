What the gap says. On the bench-fixture index `sif search search "Saltiv Protocol Notes" -n 10` ranks `Saltiv Protocol Notes` first with Score 0.1057 and `Qelron Protocol Notes` second with 0.1527; `--min-score 0.12` removes rank 1 and keeps the weaker rank 2; 0.23 and above return nothing.
