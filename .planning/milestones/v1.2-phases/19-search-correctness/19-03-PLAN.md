<objective>
Give context and staleness enrichment exactly one owner: the search pipeline's exit. Searchers stop enriching, a shared `enrich_results` helper performs the composition once, and every pipeline return path calls it exactly once.

Purpose: CORR-05 removes two to three full `contexts`/`document_embeddings` scans per hybrid query and makes the trust signals consistent by construction. The searchers become pure result producers; the pipeline is the one seam that knows every exit.
Decision coverage: implements the locked upstream "ONE context/staleness attach at pipeline exit" rule and preserves the Phase 14 D-08/D-09 marker semantics (derived staleness, two-sided context normalization).
Output: `enrich_results`, searchers without attach, a single pipeline attach per exit, and the rewritten single-owner contract tests.

Flagged assumption (spec-less probe fallback): CORR-05 was classified `unclassified`; its marker-preservation and single-owner edges are explicit planner-authored acceptance criteria.
</objective>
