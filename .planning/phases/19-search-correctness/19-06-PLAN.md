Per-chunk mode restores chunk-level rows through a fusion grouping key without touching the RRF math.

Purpose: CORR-02's core. Pages must return `limit` distinct documents even when one chunky document matches many times; the escape hatch must remain available; and the ranking-contract change must be recorded against B0 with regenerated goldens.

Output: deduped vector search, chunk identity fields, per-chunk fusion key, regenerated hybrid/RRF goldens and held-out snapshot, and the chunky-document regression.
