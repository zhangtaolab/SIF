<objective>
Enforce the persisted identity at every vector-touching boundary: search paths warn once and mark results (or fail with a remedy when vector search is impossible), embed/init paths fail fast, the three dimension guards collapse into the identity module, and query-side embedding uses the query role.

Purpose: EMB-01's public contract is the same-dimension swap protection the milestone is built around. This plan makes the identity observable where users and agents hit it, without adding a result field, changing the schema, or touching BM25-only commands.
Output: pipeline identity checks, CLI/MCP/bench/eval policy, the shared dimension read and legacy-assumed write at schema open, and role-aware query call sites.
</objective>
