<objective>
Apply the already-proven AST inside the vector leg and prove the filtered pipeline end to end. This is where the measured latent bug lives: the Phase 19 escalation loop stops on `len(rows) < fetch_k`, and `rows` is the post-JOIN result, so any selective JOIN filter — including the pre-existing collection filter — ends escalation on the first batch and silently under-returns.

Purpose: FILT-03's "adaptive over-fetch" is unimplementable without correcting the stop condition when a filter is present; the pre-count supplies the target. Fixing it is in scope, not a follow-up.
Output: the filtered vector leg with a selectivity bound and an honest notice, the hybrid passthrough contract, and the integration battery that pins the containment invariant, the 2 % recall regression, composition, and the empty-match sentinel.
</objective>
