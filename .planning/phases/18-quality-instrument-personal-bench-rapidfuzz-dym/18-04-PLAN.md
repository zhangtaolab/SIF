<objective>
Replace the difflib +/-1-first-letter bucket with a full-vocabulary rapidfuzz Indel scan at a deliberately re-measured 0-100 cutoff, as the v1.2-locked dependency change. Preserve the probe/hit contracts and the suggestion surface while removing the measured first-letter recall hole.

Purpose: QUAL-01 is a measured swap, not a library swap for its own sake. The evidence is decisive: first-letter typos recalled at 7.0% by the bucket are recalled at 100% by the full scan at cutoff 75, at ~1.7 ms/query on a 73k-term vocabulary (versus ~5.9 ms for the current bucket and ~51 ms for full-vocab difflib). This plan lands the dependency, the swap, the pinned cutoff/tie-break contracts, and the evidence tests.
Decision coverage: implements CONTEXT D-13 (`rapidfuzz>=3.14.5,<4` as a hard dependency, no difflib fallback), D-14 (full-vocabulary `fuzz.ratio` Indel scan, no first-letter bucket, cutoff re-measured to 75.0), D-15 (score desc -> term asc -> df desc tie-break), D-16 (cutoff is an internal constant, no setting/CLI knob), and D-17 (probe/hit contracts, prefix completions, and history ranking preserved).
Output: rapidfuzz in the lock, the rewritten `_close_matches`, the updated DYM test battery, and the architecture narrative.
</objective>
