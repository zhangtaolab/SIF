<objective>
Add the sweep engine and the bench orchestration that makes tuning honest.

Purpose: EVAL-03 requires a grid over weights and k where noise-sized differences do not produce a fake winner, and D-12 requires the rerank cost claim to be measurable. This plan turns the Plan 01 report surface into a ranked sweep with tie groups, deterministic ordering, embedding reuse, and off/on rerank states.
Output: `sif bench --sweep` (45 declared configs) and `sif bench --rerank` (two measured states) with table and JSON output, a pure `rank_sweep` tie-group function, and a settings-resolved baseline with deltas.
</objective>
